# 📦 Inventory & Stock Management System

A desktop-based **Inventory & Stock Management System** built with **Python, Flask, CustomTkinter, SQLAlchemy, and SQLite**. The application enables businesses to manage products, suppliers, inventory, stock transactions, sales, invoices, and reports through a modern GUI backed by a RESTful Flask API.

---

## ✨ Features

- Secure User Authentication
- Role-Based Access Control (Admin & Staff)
- Product & Category Management
- Supplier Management
- Inventory & Stock Tracking
- Stock In / Stock Out Transactions
- Low Stock Alerts
- Point-of-Sale (POS) Billing
- PDF Invoice Generation
- Excel Report Export
- Sales Analytics Dashboard
- Product Search & Filters
